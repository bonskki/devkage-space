York in October. The Minster bells at 6pm. Walking home in the rain because I missed the last bus to Heslington.

## The small things that hold you together

- A Sunday Mass that's only ten minutes' walk away.
- Cooking nasi goreng at midnight when the deadline guilt won't let you sleep.
- One WhatsApp group with friends back home that hasn't gone quiet.

## What I didn't expect

Solitude isn't the same as loneliness. Some of the clearest thinking I've done in years happened on long walks along the Ouse with no one to talk to.

The work still has to get done. But you can do hard work and still have a soul about it.
