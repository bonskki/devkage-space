When the UT Austin offer came in, I sat on it for almost a week. On paper, Austin is the obvious choice — bigger ecosystem, warmer weather, plenty of Indonesians in tech. So why York?

## The three reasons that actually mattered

1. **Viking HPC cluster access.** Real GPU time on real research hardware, not a shared university workstation.
2. **Russell Group + Global Talent Visa pathway.** The endorsement route opens up post-MSc options that the US H1B lottery doesn't.
3. **A supervisor who actually reads arXiv.** I had two calls before applying. That doesn't happen everywhere.

## What I gave up

Warm weather. A bigger Indonesian Catholic community. The comfort of a familiar accent.

But none of those move the needle on the next ten years of work.
